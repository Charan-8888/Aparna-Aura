Import Aura products

This importer converts the supplied Aparna Aura photographs into real Django Product and ProductImage records so they are delivered by the existing customer API. Alternate and model photographs are grouped into the same product gallery. Every imported product uses the requested price of ₹0.00, starts with stock 1 so it is visible as available, and can be edited normally after import.
